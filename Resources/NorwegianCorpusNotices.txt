Changes: select literal normert forms with TILDATO=4000 from the fixed 2022-02-01
full-form dictionaries (ISO-8859-1); retain lemma and fullform row identifiers.
This snapshot is not the current live normative database. Morphologically possible
dictionary forms may be very rare or never used. No original-reference words,
guessed suffixes, recased pseudo-names or invented numeric/punctuation forms.
Each written standard uses its own historical unigram data, never the other standard.
NFC/casefold token counts aggregate original case variants. Rank observed whole
forms first, then estimates from the least frequent hyphen/apostrophe/space
component when all components are observed, then unobserved forms alphabetically.
Reserve five letter-practice words, actual shortest/longest fields, maximum
hyphens/field counts/field lengths, apostrophes and Unicode-only capitals when available.
Quotas preserve aggregate input-class boundaries; this is not a pure frequency
cutoff, original-word, language-quality, difficulty or distribution equivalence.
Numbers, punctuation, spaces and case are literal source forms; no mechanical padding.
NorwegianCorpusSources.txt distinguishes whole-token counts and component estimates.
No source archives, full frequency lists, definitions, audio or images bundled.
Basic authored pools and captured historical prompts/metrics remain unchanged.
