Changes: choose 200 Bokmål and 250 Nynorsk literal normert dictionary forms
with TILDATO=4000, modern lowercase letters, lengths 1–9 and 1–10.
Use each standard's own positive whole-token unigram counts; aggregate
original case variants, never borrow counts from the other standard or
component estimates. Keep eligible earlier authored words and real length
boundaries. Two earlier Bokmål words exceed the new basic length bound;
one earlier Nynorsk word has no whole-form observation in its snapshot.
Their saved historical prompts, metrics and replay remain literal.
These fixed 2022 dictionaries are not the live normative database;
historical observations do not establish current frequency or lexical quality.
No reference words, recasing, suffix synthesis, spelling normalization or
padding. Matching candidate counts and bounds is not word-value, frequency,
difficulty or semantic equivalence. The ten scale corpora retain their own
revision and bytes. Raw dictionaries, frequency lists and media not bundled.
